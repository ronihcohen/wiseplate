# Kurdish Jewish recipes audit — 2026-09-19

## Scope and terminology

One collection page and exactly ten recipes under `content/recipes/kurdistan/`. “Kurdish” describes a region spanning parts of present-day Iraq, Turkey, Iran and Syria, not a single national canon. Kubbeh is explicitly described as an Iraqi/Kurdish/Syrian regional family; the soup forms are Jewish-Israeli continuations. Family and chef adaptations are labeled.

## Availability, kosher and safety

All ingredients are sold in Israel; frozen okra, jarred vine leaves and frozen chard are documented substitutes. Cheese kadeh is the only dairy recipe and is separated from all meat dishes. Ground beef reaches 71°C and chicken 74°C. Frying specifies 170–175°C and hot-oil supervision. Overnight dishes require adequate starting liquid, covered oven-safe pots and prompt cooling of leftovers.
