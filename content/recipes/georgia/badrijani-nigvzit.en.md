## Badrijani Nigvzit

The name means eggplant with walnuts: soft strips of [eggplant](/en/food/eggplant/) wrap a tangy paste of walnuts, garlic, cilantro and spices. It is a classic dish of the Georgian table, vegan and pareve, served at room temperature.

**Prep time:** 35 minutes · **Cooking:** 30 minutes · **Rest:** 15 minutes · **Yield:** 8 starter servings · **Difficulty:** medium

### Thin eggplant absorbs less and rolls better

Slice lengthwise 5–6 mm thick. A thick slice cracks when rolled and a very thin one dries out. Salting ahead doesn't necessarily remove "bitterness" from modern eggplant, but it reduces water and helps control the oil.

### Ingredients (for about 24 rolls)

* 1.5 kg long, firm eggplants
* Salt
* Neutral oil for frying or brushing; the calculation counts 120 grams absorbed
* 300 grams walnuts
* 4 garlic cloves
* 1 small onion, finely chopped
* 1 teaspoon ground coriander and 1/2 teaspoon ground fenugreek
* A handful of fresh cilantro
* 3 tablespoons white or red wine vinegar
* 120–180 ml water
* 100 grams pomegranate seeds, for serving

### Choosing ingredients and substitutes

Walnuts should smell sweet and nutty, not like old paint. You can bake instead of frying: brush with 80 ml oil and bake at 220°C (430°F) for 18–22 minutes, turning once. Pomegranate out of season is optional; a few herbs add color.

### Method

1. **Slice and salt.** Cut lengthwise, salt lightly and leave for 20 minutes. Blot well with paper.
2. **Fry or bake.** Shallow-fry until soft and golden and transfer to a rack, or bake as directed above. The eggplant must bend without tearing.
3. **Grind the filling.** Pulse the walnuts, garlic, onion, spices and cilantro. Add the vinegar and water gradually until you get a thick, spreadable paste. Salt.
4. **Fill.** Place a flat spoonful at the wide end of each slice and roll up without pressing the filling out.
5. **Let the flavors develop.** Arrange, cover and leave for at least 15 minutes. Scatter the pomegranate before serving.

### Variations and tips

* A filling that crumbles needs another spoonful of water; one that is too runny needs more ground walnut.
* The dish tastes better after an hour and can be made a day ahead.
* Keeps for up to 3 days in the fridge; bring back to room temperature before serving.

### Summary

Badrijani nigvzit succeeds when the eggplant is pliable and not greasy and the filling is tangy enough to balance the walnut. Three rolls are about 445 calories. See also [pkhali](/en/recipes/georgia/spinach-pkhali/) and [Georgian cuisine](/en/recipes/georgia/).

### Frequently asked questions

**Question: How many calories are in three rolls?**
Answer: About 445 when fried, based on the calculated oil absorption; about 395 when baked.

**Question: Can I make it without frying?**
Answer: Yes. Brush with oil and bake at 220°C (430°F) until the slices are soft and pliable.

**Question: Is the dish vegan?**
Answer: Yes. It is also pareve as written, but it contains nuts.

**Question: Why do the rolls crack?**
Answer: The slices are too thick or not softened enough. Slice 5–6 mm and cook until fully pliable.
