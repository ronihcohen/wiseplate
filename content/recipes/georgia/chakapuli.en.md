## Chakapuli

Chakapuli is a spring stew of lamb, white wine, plenty of tarragon and sour plum. In Georgia, green tkemali plums and their juice are used; in Israel the season is short, so the recipe gives one route with unripe green plums and another with store-bought tkemali sauce.

**Prep time:** 25 minutes · **Cooking time:** 100 minutes · **Yield:** 6 servings · **Difficulty:** medium

### Tarragon is not just a garnish

The green, anise-like flavor of tarragon is the identity of the dish. A small bunch added at the end is not enough. Add a large amount in two stages; save the delicate leaves for the finish so they don't lose their aroma.

### Ingredients (for 6 servings)

* 1.2 kg lamb shoulder or neck, cut into cubes, excess fat removed
* 2 small bunches of green onions, chopped
* 2 bunches of tarragon (about 120 grams of leaves and tender stems)
* A bunch of cilantro and a bunch of parsley
* 500 grams pitted sour green plums, or 250 ml tkemali sauce plus 250 ml water
* 375 ml dry white wine
* 4 garlic cloves, sliced
* One hot green chili, to taste
* Salt and black pepper

### Choosing ingredients and substitutes

In spring, unripe green plums are available in some markets. If they are ripe and sweet, add 2 tablespoons of lemon juice. With store-bought tkemali sauce, check the salt, sugar and kashrut label. The wine should be dry, and kosher if your kitchen requires it. Alcohol does not necessarily disappear completely during cooking.

### Method

1. **Layer.** In a heavy pot, place the lamb, green onions, half the tarragon, cilantro and parsley, the garlic and the chili.
2. **Add the sour element and liquid.** Scatter the plums and pour in the wine. On the tkemali route, pour in the sauce and water. No oil is needed.
3. **Cook gently.** Bring to a boil, cover and cook at a low simmer for 75–95 minutes, until the lamb is tender. Stir little so as not to break everything up.
4. **Balance.** Taste. If it is too sour, add a pinch of sugar; if too mild, add lemon or tkemali. Adjust the salt.
5. **Finish with green.** Add the rest of the tarragon and herbs and cook uncovered for another 5 minutes. Let stand 10 minutes before serving.

### Variations and tips

* You can make it with veal instead of lamb; the cooking time may be shorter.
* Don't brown the meat here: the flavor should stay green, tangy and bright.
* Keeps for 3 days in the fridge. Reheat gently and add a little fresh tarragon.

### Summary

Chakapuli is a contrast to the other, darker meat stews: lots of greens, fruit sourness and wine. A serving is about 610 calories. See also [chakhokhbili](/en/recipes/georgia/chakhokhbili/) and [Georgian cuisine](/en/recipes/georgia/).

### Frequently asked questions

**Question: What can I use instead of tkemali plums?**
Answer: Unripe green plums, or store-bought tkemali sauce with water. Ripe plums need a boost of lemon.

**Question: How many calories are in a serving?**
Answer: One sixth of the pot is about 610 calories, before bread.

**Question: Can I make it without wine?**
Answer: You can replace it with chicken broth or water plus a tablespoon of lemon, but the flavor will be less complex.

**Question: Can I replace the tarragon?**
Answer: Not entirely. You can supplement with a little dill or parsley, but at least one large bunch of tarragon is needed for the dish's identity.
