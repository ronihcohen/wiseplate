## Lobio

In Georgian, *lobio* (in Hebrew "לוביו") means beans, and the name covers many versions. This is a thick version of partly mashed red beans with onion, walnuts, cilantro and fenugreek — a vegan meal you can serve hot or at room temperature.

**Prep time:** 20 minutes · **Soaking:** overnight · **Cooking:** 90 minutes · **Yield:** 6 servings · **Difficulty:** easy

### Mash only some of the beans

Starch from the mashed beans thickens the pot, while the whole beans keep the texture. If you blend everything you get a heavy spread; if you don't mash at all you get a thin soup.

### Ingredients (for 6 servings)

* 500 grams dried red beans, soaked 8–12 hours
* 2 medium onions, chopped
* 3 tablespoons oil
* 100 grams ground walnuts
* 4 garlic cloves, crushed
* 1 teaspoon ground coriander, 1/2 teaspoon ground fenugreek and 1/2 teaspoon chili
* 1 bunch cilantro, chopped
* 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
* Salt and black pepper

### Choosing ingredients and substitutes

You can use about 1.2 kg of drained canned beans and cook them for 25 minutes with 500 ml of water. Khmeli suneli from a spice shop can replace the coriander and fenugreek, but check the salt. Walnuts are a major allergen and there is no substitute that gives the same flavor.

### Method

1. **Cook the beans.** Drain the soaking water, cover with fresh water and bring to a boil. Simmer gently for 60–90 minutes, until the beans are completely soft. Reserve two cups of the cooking water.
2. **Fry the onion.** Heat the oil in a pot, add the onion and a pinch of salt and fry for 10–12 minutes until golden.
3. **Bloom the spices.** Add the garlic, ground coriander, fenugreek and chili and stir for half a minute.
4. **Combine.** Add the beans, walnuts and one cup of the cooking water. Mash about a quarter to a third of the beans and cook for 15 minutes. Add liquid as needed.
5. **Balance.** Turn off the heat, add the cilantro and vinegar, adjust the salt and let the pot stand for 15 minutes before serving.

### Variations and tips

* Don't add vinegar before the beans are soft; acid can slow the softening.
* If the pot dries out, add hot cooking water, not oil.
* Keeps 4 days in the refrigerator. When reheating, thin it a little because the stew thickens.

### Summary

Good lobio sits between a stew and a spread: some of the beans stay whole and some turn into sauce. A serving is about 465 calories and plenty of fiber. Serve it alongside [pkhali](/en/recipes/georgia/spinach-pkhali/) and see the rest of [Georgian cuisine](/en/recipes/georgia/).

### Frequently asked questions

**Question: How many calories are in lobio?**
Answer: One sixth of the pot is about 465 calories, before bread.

**Question: Can you make it with canned beans?**
Answer: Yes. Use about 1.2 kg drained and cook with water for about 25 minutes.

**Question: Is lobio vegan?**
Answer: This recipe is vegan and pareve. Check bread and sides separately.

**Question: Why did the beans stay hard?**
Answer: They were old, the water was hard, or the vinegar went in too early. Cook until soft and only then add vinegar.
