## Spinach Pkhali

Pkhali (in Hebrew "פחאלי") is the name for a family of dishes of chopped vegetables mixed with walnuts, garlic, herbs and vinegar. It can be made from beets, green beans or cabbage; the spinach version is green, fresh and especially common. It is served as flattened balls or as a spread, usually with pomegranate seeds.

**Prep time:** 25 minutes · **Cooking:** 5 minutes · **Chilling:** 30 minutes · **Yield:** 8 appetizer servings · **Difficulty:** easy

### Squeezing determines the texture

Cooked spinach holds a lot of water. If you don't really squeeze it by hand, the walnuts won't be able to bind the mixture and it will turn into a puddle. From 1.2 kg of fresh spinach, a much smaller amount remains — that is normal.

### Ingredients (for 8 servings)

* 1.2 kg fresh spinach, or 700 grams thawed frozen spinach
* 300 grams walnuts
* 4 garlic cloves
* 1 small onion, chopped very finely
* 1 bunch cilantro
* 1 teaspoon ground coriander and 1/2 teaspoon ground fenugreek
* 3 tablespoons white wine vinegar
* Salt, black pepper and a pinch of chili
* 100 grams pomegranate seeds

### Choosing ingredients and substitutes

Turkish spinach or baby spinach work, but tough stems need to be removed. For frozen spinach, choose a product with no sauce or seasoning and squeeze it after thawing. Walnuts are an allergen, and the dish should not be served as a "spinach spread" without mentioning them.

### Method

1. **Blanch.** Put the spinach in a wide pot with the water left from rinsing, cover and cook for 3–5 minutes just until it collapses. Cool immediately.
2. **Squeeze and chop.** Squeeze handfuls hard until almost no water comes out and chop finely with a knife. There is no need to turn it into a puree.
3. **Grind the walnuts.** Grind the walnuts, garlic, half of the cilantro, the spices and the vinegar into a coarse paste.
4. **Mix.** Combine the spinach, walnut paste, onion and the rest of the cilantro. Taste for salt and vinegar. Chill for 30 minutes.
5. **Shape.** Form 16 small balls, flatten them slightly and make a dimple with your thumb. Garnish with pomegranate.

### Variations and tips

* For beet pkhali, replace the spinach with 1 kg of roasted, grated and squeezed beets.
* The mixture improves after a few hours and keeps 3 days refrigerated.
* If it is soft, chill it before adding more walnuts; cold firms up the fat.

### Summary

Pkhali is not a spinach salad but a balance of squeezed greens, walnut, garlic and vinegar. Two large balls are about 295 calories. Serve alongside [badrijani](/en/recipes/georgia/badrijani-nigvzit/) and see [Georgian cuisine](/en/recipes/georgia/).

### Frequently asked questions

**Question: How many calories are in pkhali?**
Answer: One eighth of the batch, about two large balls, is about 295 calories.

**Question: Can you use frozen spinach?**
Answer: Yes. Thaw 700 grams and squeeze well; there is no need to blanch again.

**Question: Is pkhali vegan?**
Answer: Yes, the recipe is vegan and pareve, but it contains a large amount of walnuts.

**Question: Why won't the mixture hold its shape?**
Answer: The spinach stayed wet or the mixture is warm. Squeeze well and chill before shaping.
