## Bukharian Non

Non, also called lepyoshka in Israel, is a round bread with a thick rim and a flat, pricked center. It is a widespread Central Asian bread, not a solely Jewish one, but it was an everyday part of the Bukharian Jewish table.

**Prep time:** 30 minutes · **Rising:** 90 minutes · **Baking:** 20 minutes · **Yield:** 8 · **Difficulty:** medium

### A thin center that won't puff up

The traditional bread stamp is called a chekich. At home, use a fork and prick the center densely, leaving a thick ring. A well-preheated stone or upside-down sheet pan imitates some of the heat of a tandoor.

### Ingredients (for 8 loaves)

* 1 kg white flour
* 12 grams dry yeast
* 20 grams salt, 15 grams sugar
* 600–650 ml lukewarm water
* 30 ml oil
* 25 grams sesame or nigella seeds, optional

### Choosing ingredients and substitutes

Bread flour gives a chewy rim, but regular white flour works. You don't need a tandoor: preheat a pizza stone or a thick metal pan for 40 minutes. Use baking paper only if it is rated for 250°C (480°F).

### Method

1. Knead the flour, yeast, sugar, salt, water and oil for 10 minutes. Let rise for an hour.
2. Divide into 8 balls and rest for 20 minutes. Heat the oven and stone to 250°C (480°F).
3. Open each ball into an 18 cm round. Leave a thick rim and press the center to 5 mm thick.
4. Prick the center densely, moisten lightly and sprinkle with seeds.
5. Bake 2–3 at a time for 7–9 minutes until the rims are golden with charred spots. Cool on a rack.

### Variations and tips

* For small portions, divide into 12; each loaf will be about 325 calories.
* A center that puffs up was not pricked enough or is too thick.
* Freeze after cooling and reheat straight in the oven.

### Frequently asked questions

**Question: How many calories are in Bukharian non?**
Answer: A small loaf out of eight is about 490 calories.

**Question: Are non and lepyoshka the same thing?**
Answer: In Israel the names are sometimes used for the same round Central Asian bread.

**Question: Do I need a special stamp?**
Answer: No. A fork makes effective holes, even if the pattern is less decorative.

**Question: Can I use whole wheat flour?**
Answer: Replace up to 30% and add a little water; more than that will give a heavier bread.
