## Bukharian Chopped Salad

In Bukharian restaurants in Israel, a finely chopped salad of tomato, cucumber, onion and herbs is served alongside oshpelo and meat pastries. It is close to Uzbekistan's achichuk salads and to Israeli salad; the addition of cucumber and oil reflects a common local version, not a sharp historical boundary.

**Prep time:** 20 minutes · **Yield:** 6 servings · **Difficulty:** easy

### Chop just before serving

5 mm cubes give an even texture. Salt draws out liquid, so season at the last moment. With watery tomatoes, remove some of the seeds, but not all the juice.

### Ingredients (for 6 servings)

* 800 grams firm [tomatoes](/en/food/tomato/)
* 500 grams [cucumbers](/en/food/cucumber/)
* 200 grams red onion
* Half a bunch of cilantro and half a bunch of parsley
* 45 ml [olive oil](/en/food/olive-oil/)
* Juice of one lemon
* Salt, black pepper and a pinch of cumin

### Choosing ingredients and substitutes

Small Israeli cucumbers stay crunchy. If you want a version closer to achichuk, leave out the cucumber and add hot pepper. Wash and dry the herbs well.

### Method

1. Chop the tomatoes and cucumbers into small, even cubes.
2. Slice the onion thinly; for a milder bite, rinse it in cold water and dry it.
3. Chop the herbs with a sharp knife and mix with the vegetables.
4. Just before serving, add the oil, lemon, salt, pepper and cumin and mix once.
5. Serve immediately; a salad that has stood releases liquid but is still safe refrigerated for up to a day.

### Variations and tips

* Without oil the salad is about 60 calories per serving.
* Hot green pepper works, but add a little and taste.
* Don't chop in a food processor; it crushes the vegetables and releases liquid.

### Summary

A good Israeli Bukharian salad is fine, crunchy and seasoned at the last moment. One serving is about 125 calories. ### Frequently asked questions

**Question: How many calories are in Bukharian salad?**
Answer: A serving with olive oil is about 125 calories.

**Question: Is it the same as Israeli salad?**
Answer: There is a lot of overlap. Here the context, the fine chopping and serving it alongside Bukharian food define the version.

**Question: Is cucumber a must?**
Answer: No. A classic Central Asian achichuk salad is sometimes based only on tomato and onion.

**Question: Can I make it ahead?**
Answer: You can chop it two hours ahead and keep it cold, but dress it only when serving.
