## Osh Savo — Bukharian Shabbat Stew

Osh savo means roughly "Shabbat rice." A Central Asian pilaf was adapted to the prohibition on cooking on Shabbat and became a Bukharian Jewish overnight pot — a hamin, the Shabbat stew left to cook overnight. There are dry and moist versions; here the rice is soft and moist, with tomatoes, apple and dried fruit.

**Prep time:** 35 minutes · **Cooking:** 9–10 hours · **Yield:** 8 · **Difficulty:** medium

### Enough liquid for the night

Rice keeps absorbing for hours. At the start of baking, the liquid should be about 2 cm above the rice. Use a heavy pot with an ovenproof lid and a steady oven; don't improvise a plastic lid or an unsafe hot plate.

### Ingredients (for 8 servings)

* 500 grams long-grain rice, rinsed
* 1 kg beef for long cooking, in large cubes
* 80 ml oil
* 3 onions, sliced
* 500 grams chopped tomatoes
* 400 grams apples, in wedges
* 150 grams dried apricots, 150 grams prunes, 150 grams pitted dates
* 240 grams cooked chickpeas
* 1 teaspoon cumin, 1 teaspoon black pepper, 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon, salt
* 1.4–1.7 liters boiling water

### Choosing ingredients and substitutes

Chuck, neck or shoulder work; a cut with a little collagen stays juicy. The amount and type of fruit vary between families. You can cut them by half for a less sweet pot and about 90 fewer calories per serving.

### Method

1. Heat the oil in an ovenproof pot, brown the meat and remove it. Brown the onion for 10 minutes.
2. Add the tomatoes and spices and cook for 5 minutes. Return the meat and add the chickpeas and fruit.
3. Scatter the rice over and add boiling water to 2 cm above it. Boil for 5 minutes.
4. Cover tightly and move to an oven preheated to 110°C (230°F) for 9–10 hours.
5. In the morning, check that the meat is tender and the pot is very hot in the center. If it dries out early, add boiling water; never cold water to a hot pot.

### Variations and tips

* For a drier pot, start with 1.4 liters; for a moister one, 1.7.
* Generous black pepper balances the sweetness of the fruit.
* Refrigerate leftovers within two hours in shallow containers.

### Frequently asked questions

**Question: How many calories are in osh savo?**
Answer: A serving out of eight is about 835 calories.

**Question: Do I need all the fruit?**
Answer: No. The versions are very family-specific; you can pick two kinds and reduce the sweetness.

**Question: Is it the same as tbit?**
Answer: No. Both are rice Shabbat stews, but the seasoning, the fruit and the community tradition differ.

**Question: Can I use a slow cooker?**
Answer: Yes, if the manufacturer approves it, after a full boil on the stovetop and on a heat setting that keeps food safely hot.
