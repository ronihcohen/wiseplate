## Gomen — Ethiopian Greens

Gomen is the name for cooked leafy greens, and sometimes for the stew itself. In Ethiopia local varieties of collard greens are used; in Israel, kale, Swiss chard or mustard greens give an accessible result. It is a mild side that balances a spicy wat.

**Prep time:** 20 minutes · **Cooking time:** 30 minutes · **Yield:** 6 servings · **Difficulty:** easy

### Stems and leaves at different times

Kale and chard stems are tougher than the leaves. Chop them finely and cook them with the onion; add the leaves later. That way you don't end up with tough stems and gray leaves.

### Ingredients (for 6 servings)

* 1.2 kg kale, Swiss chard or a mix, washed well
* 60 ml olive oil
* 200 grams chopped onion
* 4 garlic cloves and 20 grams ginger, chopped
* One hot green chili, optional
* 1/2 teaspoon turmeric, salt and pepper
* 100–150 ml water
* Juice of half a [lemon](/en/food/lemon/)

### Choosing ingredients and substitutes

Kale gives a texture close to collard greens; chard is softer and cooks faster. Spinach has a lot of water and shrinks a great deal, so you need 1.5 kg and a shorter cooking time. Wash the leaves twice in a bowl of water to remove sand.

### Method

1. **Separate.** Remove the thick stems, chop them finely and cut the leaves into wide strips.
2. **Fry the base.** Heat the oil, add the onion and stems and cook for 8 minutes. Add the garlic, ginger, chili and turmeric for a minute.
3. **Wilt the leaves.** Add the leaves in several batches and stir until they collapse. Add the water, cover and cook for 12–20 minutes depending on the type of leaf.
4. **Finish.** Remove the lid and evaporate excess liquid. Salt and add the lemon. The leaves should be tender but not mush.

### Variations and tips

* Alongside kitfo, keep the gomen mild and don't add berbere.
* Chard needs about 12 minutes; mature kale may need 20.
* Keeps for 4 days in the fridge and freezes well for up to two months.

### Summary

Good gomen is tender but still green, with stems cooked ahead and gentle seasoning. A serving is about 170 calories. It goes well alongside [kitfo](/en/recipes/ethiopia/kitfo/) or [misir wat](/en/recipes/ethiopia/misir-wat/).

### Frequently asked questions

**Question: How many calories are in gomen?**
Answer: A serving out of six is about 170 calories, including 10 ml of oil per person.

**Question: Kale or Swiss chard?**
Answer: Both work. Kale is sturdy and closer to collard greens; chard is delicate and faster.

**Question: Why did the gomen come out watery?**
Answer: The leaves were not drained or the pot stayed covered at the end. Remove the lid and evaporate the liquid before the final seasoning.

**Question: Is gomen spicy?**
Answer: It is usually a mild side. The green chili is optional and there is no need for berbere.
