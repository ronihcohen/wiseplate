## Atkilt Wat

Atkilt means vegetables, and the version familiar from restaurants in Israel combines cabbage, carrots and [potatoes](/en/food/baked-potato/) in a mild yellow alicha seasoning. It is not a dish of one region or religion, but a widespread home-style side that suits a shared platter.

**Prep time:** 20 minutes · **Cooking time:** 40 minutes · **Yield:** 6 servings · **Difficulty:** easy

### Cut according to cooking time

Potatoes into 2 cm cubes, carrots into thin slices and cabbage into wide strips. If they are all the same size, the cabbage will fall apart before the potato softens.

### Ingredients (for 6 servings)

* 800 grams potatoes, peeled and cubed
* 800 grams white [cabbage](/en/food/cabbage/), cut into strips
* 400 grams sliced [carrots](/en/food/carrot/)
* 200 grams chopped onion
* 60 ml olive or canola oil
* 4 garlic cloves and 20 grams ginger, chopped
* 1 teaspoon turmeric, 1/2 teaspoon cumin, salt and pepper
* 350 ml hot water

### Choosing ingredients and substitutes

Yellow or red potatoes hold their shape. You can replace a third of the cabbage with cauliflower, but add it with the cabbage. There is no need for [berbere](/en/recipes/ethiopia/awaze/): this is an alicha dish, and the turmeric is what gives it color.

### Method

1. **Soften the onion.** Heat the oil and cook the onion for 8 minutes. Add the garlic, ginger, turmeric and cumin for a minute.
2. **Start with the hard vegetables.** Add the potatoes and carrots, stir and add 250 ml water. Cover and cook for 15 minutes.
3. **Add the cabbage.** Add the cabbage, salt and the remaining 100 ml of water, cover and cook for another 15–20 minutes. Stir gently once or twice.
4. **Reduce.** Remove the lid and evaporate excess liquid for 5 minutes. The vegetables should be tender and whole, not soup.

### Variations and tips

* Don't stir much after adding the potatoes so they don't turn into mash.
* For a spicy version, serve berbere on the side and don't change the alicha base.
* Keeps for 4 days in the fridge; reheat covered with a spoonful of water.

### Summary

Atkilt wat is the mild counterpart to berbere stews: turmeric, ginger and vegetables that stay whole. A serving is about 260 calories. Serve alongside [shiro](/en/recipes/ethiopia/shiro-wat/) and [gomen](/en/recipes/ethiopia/gomen/).

### Frequently asked questions

**Question: How many calories are in atkilt wat?**
Answer: A serving out of six is about 260 calories without injera.

**Question: Why is there no berbere?**
Answer: This is a mild alicha version that gets its color from turmeric. Not every wat is red or spicy.

**Question: How do I keep the potatoes from crumbling?**
Answer: Cut them into even cubes, cook at a gentle simmer and stir little.

**Question: Can I make it ahead?**
Answer: Yes. The flavor keeps well for two or three days, but reheat gently to preserve the shape of the vegetables.
