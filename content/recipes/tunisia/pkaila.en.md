## Pkaila

Pkaila is a signature stew of Tunisian Jews: [spinach](/en/food/spinach/) cooked until it loses its water and fried in oil until very dark, then simmered with white beans and beef. The color is almost black, but the flavor isn't burnt; it is deep, green and slightly sweet.

**Prep time:** 35 minutes · **Cooking time:** 3.5 hours · **Yield:** 8 servings · **Difficulty:** Medium

### Dark is not burnt

The spinach has to dry out before frying. Fry over medium-low heat and stir often. If it browns in patches and smokes, the heat is too high; if it stays watery, it will stew instead of fry.

### Ingredients (for 8 servings)

* 1.5 kg fresh spinach, or 1 kg frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed
* 180 ml olive oil
* 800 g beef for long cooking, cubed, from Israeli cut 5 or 8 (chuck/shoulder)
* 300 g dried white beans, soaked 12 hours and drained
* 1 large onion, chopped, and 6 [garlic](/en/food/garlic/) cloves
* 100 g tomato paste
* 1 tablespoon harissa, 1 teaspoon paprika, 1 teaspoon ground coriander, 1/2 teaspoon caraway
* 1.5 liters hot water, salt and pepper

### Choosing ingredients and substitutes

Frozen spinach without sauce is a good, easy-to-find substitute; squeeze it until almost no liquid is left. You can make the spinach base ahead and freeze it. Canned beans aren't suited to the full cooking time; if you must, add 700 g drained only in the last hour.

### Method

1. **Dry the spinach.** Steam the spinach in a wide pot without oil until the liquid evaporates, 20–30 minutes, and let cool slightly.
2. **Make the pkaila base.** Add 140 ml of the oil and fry over medium-low heat 45–60 minutes, stirring, until the spinach is very dark and crumbly. Don't let it smoke.
3. **Sear the meat.** In a heavy pot heat the remaining oil, brown the beef cubes and remove. Fry the onion and garlic.
4. **Bloom the spices.** Add the tomato paste, harissa and spices and fry a minute. Return the meat and add the beans, spinach base and water.
5. **Cook slowly.** Bring to a boil, partially cover and cook 2.5–3 hours at a low simmer, until the beans and meat are tender. Add hot water as needed.
6. **Adjust and serve.** Salt only when the beans are almost tender, then cook another 15 minutes. The beef should reach 71°C (160°F) and be completely tender.

### Variations and tips

* The spinach base keeps up to three months in the freezer, divided into containers.
* Pkaila is thick but not dry; add only boiling water so the cooking doesn't stop.
* Cool within two hours and keep up to 4 days in the fridge. Reheat until fully bubbling.

### Summary

The signature of pkaila is slow, controlled frying of squeezed spinach, not burning it. A serving is about 580 calories. For a completely different Tunisian holiday stew, see [msoki](/en/recipes/tunisia/msoki/).

### Frequently asked questions

**Question: How many calories are in pkaila?**
Answer: One serving from a pot of eight is about 580 calories, including all the olive oil.

**Question: Is the spinach supposed to be black?**
Answer: Very dark, almost black, but not charred. Work over medium-low heat and stir.

**Question: Can I use frozen spinach?**
Answer: Yes. Thaw and squeeze it well; excess water greatly lengthens the frying stage.

**Question: Can it be made a day ahead?**
Answer: Yes. The flavor improves after a night, and you reheat it gently with a little water.
