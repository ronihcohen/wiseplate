## Tunisian Lablabi

Lablabi is a Tunisian [chickpea](/en/food/chickpeas/) soup assembled right in the bowl: day-old bread torn by hand, topped with chickpeas and their broth, then harissa, garlic, cumin, lemon and olive oil. Egg and tuna turn it into a complete meal.

**Prep time:** 20 minutes · **Soaking:** 8 hours · **Cooking:** 90 minutes · **Yield:** 8 bowls · **Difficulty:** Easy

### The bread is part of the dish

The bread isn't served on the side. It is torn into the bowl so it soaks up the chickpea liquid while still keeping a bit of texture. A day-old baguette or plain loaf works better than fresh, soft bread.

### Ingredients (for 8 servings)

* 400 g dried chickpeas, soaked 8–12 hours and drained
* 2 liters water, plus more as needed
* 8 garlic cloves, half of them crushed
* 2 teaspoons cumin, 1 teaspoon caraway, salt
* 400 g day-old bread, torn
* 8 eggs
* 240 g tuna in water, drained weight
* 3 tablespoons harissa
* 50 ml olive oil
* 2 lemons, juiced; parsley and capers for serving

### Choosing ingredients and substitutes

You can take a shortcut with 1 kg of drained canned chickpeas and 1.2 liters of water: cook 25 minutes with the garlic and spices. Plain Israeli bread works; whole-wheat bread adds fiber but tastes less traditional.

### Method

1. **Cook the chickpeas.** Cover the soaked chickpeas with fresh water by 5 cm, bring to a boil and skim the foam. Add four garlic cloves and cook 75–100 minutes until very soft. Salt at the end.
2. **Cook the eggs.** For hard-boiled eggs, cook 10 minutes and cool. For a soft version use pasteurized eggs; serve hard-boiled eggs to at-risk groups.
3. **Season the soup.** Add the cumin and caraway to the pot and mash one ladleful of chickpeas to thicken. The soup should stay brothy.
4. **Build the bowls.** Divide the bread, ladle over very hot chickpeas and broth, and add the tuna and egg.
5. **Finish.** Mix the harissa, the remaining garlic, lemon and a little broth. Divide among the bowls and drizzle with olive oil. Serve immediately.

### Variations and tips

* Serve the harissa on the side when diners like different levels of heat.
* The chickpeas and broth can be frozen; add the bread, egg and tuna after reheating.
* Cooked chickpeas keep 4 days in the fridge. Bring back to a boil before serving.

### Summary

Lablabi is more than chickpea soup: the soaked bread and toppings are built in the bowl to taste. A full bowl is about 480 calories. For a Tunisian sandwich with the same tuna and harissa flavors, see [fricassé](/en/recipes/tunisia/fricasse/).

### Frequently asked questions

**Question: How many calories are in lablabi?**
Answer: A bowl with bread, egg, tuna and oil is about 480 calories; without the egg and tuna, about 375.

**Question: Can I use canned chickpeas?**
Answer: Yes. Cook them 25 minutes with water, garlic and spices to create a full-flavored broth.

**Question: Why is bread put inside the soup?**
Answer: It's the traditional way of serving it; the day-old bread thickens the bowl and turns the soup into a meal.

**Question: Can it be made vegan?**
Answer: Yes. Leave out the tuna and egg and add more chickpeas, capers and olive oil to taste.
