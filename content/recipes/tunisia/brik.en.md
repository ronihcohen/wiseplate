## Tunisian Brik

Brik is a thin malsouka sheet wrapped around an [egg](/en/food/eggs/), tuna and potato that goes straight into the oil. In Israel, a round cigar pastry sheet is the convenient substitute: it is thin, doesn't tear easily and folds into a half-moon. The secret is to have the filling and the pan ready before you crack the egg.

**Prep time:** 25 minutes · **Frying time:** 20 minutes · **Yield:** 8 pieces · **Difficulty:** Medium

### How to keep the egg inside the sheet

Make a low ring of potato that holds the egg in the center. Wet only the edges of the sheet, fold and slide it into the oil immediately. A brik left waiting on the counter will go soft and leak.

### Ingredients (for 8 pieces)

* 8 large round cigar pastry sheets, about 240 g in total
* 300 g cooked, mashed potatoes
* 240 g tuna in water, drained weight
* 8 small eggs
* 1 green onion, chopped, and a handful of chopped parsley
* 2 tablespoons drained capers, 1 teaspoon harissa
* Salt, black pepper and a little ground caraway
* Oil for frying; about 80 g absorbed was counted in the calculation
* Lemon wedges for serving

### Choosing ingredients and substitutes

Frozen cigar sheets are available in Israeli supermarket chains; thaw them in their package so they don't dry out. Phyllo sheets are more fragile and need two layers. You can leave out the tuna, but then add another 200 g of potato and season well.

### Method

1. **Set up a station.** Mix the potato, tuna, herbs, capers, harissa and spices. Heat oil 2 cm deep to 175°C (347°F).
2. **Arrange the filling.** Lay a sheet on a plate, put one-eighth of the filling on one half and make a wide well.
3. **Crack the egg.** Crack an egg into a small bowl, slide it into the well and wet the edges of the sheet with water.
4. **Fold and fry.** Fold into a half-moon and slide into the oil. Fry 60–90 seconds per side for a soft egg, or 2 minutes per side for a well-done egg.
5. **Drain and serve.** Transfer to a rack and serve immediately with lemon. For children, pregnant women, older adults or people who are immunocompromised, cook until the yolk is firm; the safe target for egg is 71°C (160°F).

### Variations and tips

* Want a runny yolk? Use pasteurized eggs and keep your work surface clean.
* You can bake at 210°C after brushing with oil, but the egg is harder to control and the sheet blisters less.
* Don't store fried brik: steam softens the pastry within minutes.

### Summary

Tunisian brik is a game of timing: a thawed sheet, a ready filling and hot oil before the egg is cracked. One piece is about 320 calories. For a lighter dish from the same world of flavors, see [slata mechouia](/en/recipes/tunisia/slata-mechouia/).

### Frequently asked questions

**Question: How many calories are in a brik?**
Answer: One brik with egg, tuna and potato is about 320 calories; the amount depends mainly on the oil and the weight of the sheet.

**Question: What replaces malsouka in Israel?**
Answer: A round cigar pastry sheet. It is close in thickness and behavior; phyllo works only in two layers.

**Question: Why did the brik open up?**
Answer: The sheet dried out, was overfilled or sat waiting after folding. Keep the sheets covered and fry each piece right away.

**Question: Does the yolk have to stay runny?**
Answer: No. That's the traditional preference, but for at-risk groups cook until the egg is completely firm.
