## Tunisian Slata Mechouia

"Mechouia" means grilled: peppers, tomatoes, onion and garlic are charred, peeled and chopped by hand to a coarse texture. Tuna, [egg](/en/food/eggs/) and olives aren't just garnish; they turn the salad into a generous starter.

**Prep time:** 25 minutes · **Roasting time:** 35 minutes · **Yield:** 6 servings · **Difficulty:** Easy

### Chop, don't grind

A food processor turns the vegetables into a watery spread. A knife or a vegetable masher leaves small pieces and a smoky texture. Let the peeled vegetables drain before chopping.

### Ingredients (for 6 servings)

* 8 sweet green or red peppers
* 2 hot green peppers, to taste
* 5 tomatoes and 1 onion, halved
* 1 small head of garlic, halved
* 6 tablespoons olive oil
* 1 teaspoon ground coriander, 1/2 teaspoon caraway, salt and pepper
* Juice of one lemon
* 300 g tuna in water, drained weight
* 4 hard-boiled eggs, quartered
* 100 g black or green olives

### Choosing ingredients and substitutes

A light green, elongated pepper gives a characteristic Tunisian flavor, but Israeli bell peppers are easy to find and sweeter. To roast without a grill, use a very hot oven. Tuna in oil raises the calories depending on how much oil remains.

### Method

1. **Roast the vegetables.** Heat the oven to 240°C (464°F) or a grill. Arrange the peppers, tomatoes, onion and garlic and roast 25–35 minutes, turning the peppers, until charred and soft.
2. **Steam and peel.** Cover the peppers in a bowl for 10 minutes, then peel and seed them. Peel the tomatoes and garlic. Don't rinse — water washes away flavor.
3. **Drain and chop.** Set in a colander for 10 minutes, then chop everything by hand to a coarse texture.
4. **Season.** Mix with four tablespoons of the oil, the spices and lemon. Chill at least 20 minutes.
5. **Serve.** Arrange the tuna, eggs and olives on top and drizzle with two tablespoons of oil. Keep refrigerated, and don't leave the tuna and egg out for more than two hours.

### Variations and tips

* For a mild salad, leave out the hot pepper and serve harissa on the side.
* Make the vegetable base a day ahead; add the tuna and egg when serving.
* Keeps up to 3 days in the fridge in a closed container.

### Summary

Slata mechouia gets its character from real charring and coarse chopping, not long cooking. A serving with tuna and egg is about 340 calories. It goes well alongside [fish couscous](/en/recipes/tunisia/fish-couscous/) or as an extra filling for fricassé.

### Frequently asked questions

**Question: How many calories are in slata mechouia?**
Answer: A serving with tuna, egg, olives and oil is about 340 calories; the vegetable base alone is about 185.

**Question: Do I have to peel the peppers?**
Answer: It's recommended. The charred skin is tough and bitter; steam them in a covered bowl to remove it easily.

**Question: Can I grind it in a food processor?**
Answer: Better not. A few short pulses are possible, but hand chopping gives the right texture and doesn't release too much liquid.

**Question: Does the salad have to be spicy?**
Answer: No. The number of hot peppers is flexible, and you can serve harissa separately.
