## Yemenite Kubaneh

Kubaneh (in Hebrew "קובנה") is a rich yeast bread baked in a covered pot. In different families it is smooth, pull-apart or rolled; here we make twisted kubaneh, rolls separated from one another by clarified butter. Unlike jachnun, the crumb is fluffy and soft rather than dense and layered.

**Prep time:** 45 minutes · **Proofing:** about 2 hours · **Baking:** 8 hours · **Yield:** 12 rolls · **Difficulty:** medium

### Butter without excess water

Yemenite samneh is not exactly the same as commercial ghee, but clarified butter is the most accessible Israeli substitute. Regular butter works for a short bake, but in an overnight bake the milk solids may brown too fast.

### Ingredients (for 12 rolls)

* 700 grams white flour
* 10 grams dry yeast
* 70 grams sugar
* 14 grams salt
* 430–460 ml lukewarm water
* 140 grams clarified butter or ghee, softened

### Choosing ingredients and substitutes

Regular white flour gives a tender crumb; bread flour gives more chew. For a pareve (dairy-free) version you can use a good margarine, but that is an Israeli adaptation and the flavor is different. A 24 cm jachnun pot or a heavy ovenproof pot with a lid works.

### Method

1. **Knead.** Knead the flour, yeast, sugar, salt and 430 ml of water for 10 minutes into a soft dough. Add water as needed.
2. **First rise.** Cover and let rise for 60–75 minutes, until almost doubled.
3. **Shape.** Divide into 12 pieces. With greased hands, open each piece into a rectangle, spread 10–11 grams of clarified butter (about 2 teaspoons), roll into one log and leave it whole. Save the rest of the butter for greasing the pot and the top.
4. **Second rise.** Arrange in a greased pot with a little space between them, cover and let rise for 35–45 minutes.
5. **Bake.** Bake for 30 minutes at 180 degrees Celsius (355°F), lower to 100 degrees Celsius (210°F) and bake covered for 7–8 hours. Don't leave plastic handles in the oven.
6. **Cool and turn out.** Cool for 15 minutes before turning out. Refrigerate leftovers within two hours.

### Variations and tips

* For same-day baking: bake covered for 50 minutes at 180 degrees Celsius (355°F) and another 10 minutes uncovered.
* If the bottom is too dark, set the pot on a doubled baking sheet.
* Baked rolls can be frozen for up to a month and reheated covered.

### Summary

Good kubaneh is a soft, fluffy, gently buttered bread, not a dense lump of dough. One roll is about 320 calories. For a quick pan-fried dough from the same world, see [malawach](/en/recipes/yemen/malawach/).

### Frequently asked questions

**Question: How many calories are in kubaneh?**
Answer: One roll out of 12 is about 320 calories.

**Question: Why didn't the kubaneh rise?**
Answer: The yeast may be old, the water too hot or the dough cold. Proof by volume, not just by the clock.

**Question: Does kubaneh need to bake overnight?**
Answer: No. A short bake gives excellent bread, but overnight baking gives a dark crust and a deep flavor.

**Question: Can you use oil instead of butter?**
Answer: You can, but the separation between the rolls and the aroma will be different. A solid or clarified fat is better.
