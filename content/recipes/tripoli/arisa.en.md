## Arisa — Libyan Wheat Sabbath Stew

Don't confuse this *arisa* with the spicy paste *harissa*. The arisa of Libyan Jews is a red [wheat](/en/food/gluten/) Sabbath stew (a hamin, or cholent) made with tomato paste and paprika, with meat, chickpeas, potatoes and eggs. The wheat absorbs the meat juices through the night and becomes soft and sticky but keeps its kernel.

**Prep time:** 25 minutes · **Cook time:** 10–12 hours · **Total:** overnight · **Yield:** 8 servings · **Difficulty:** easy

### The water ratio is the difference between a juicy stew and a dry lump

Whole wheat absorbs roughly twice its weight in water, and overnight cooking evaporates more liquid. Start with the liquid 3–4 cm above the contents, check after an hour, and only then move it to overnight heat. The pot should be heavy and well covered.

### Ingredients (for 8 servings)

* 500 grams whole wheat berries, rinsed and soaked for 8 hours
* 800 grams [beef](/en/food/red-meat/), Israeli cut no. 8 or no. 5, in large pieces
* 2 tablespoons [olive oil](/en/food/olive-oil/)
* 1 large [onion](/en/food/onion/), chopped
* 150 grams [tomato](/en/food/tomato/) paste
* 1 tablespoon sweet paprika, 1 teaspoon caraway, 1/2 teaspoon hot paprika
* 250 grams cooked chickpeas, drained
* 800 grams small potatoes, peeled
* 6–8 [eggs](/en/food/eggs/) in their shells, washed
* 2–2.5 liters boiling water, salt and pepper

### Choosing ingredients and substitutes

Wheat for hamin is sold next to the legumes in every supermarket in Israel. Don't use bulgur, which has already been cooked and will fall apart. Cut no. 8 gives gelatin and a rich sauce; no. 5 is slightly leaner. Canned chickpeas work, because the long cooking will soften them a lot anyway.

### Method

1. **Brown the meat and onion.** Heat oil in a heavy pot, brown the meat on all sides and add the onion until golden.
2. **Fry the paste and spices.** Add the paste, paprika and caraway and stir for 2–3 minutes until the paste darkens.
3. **Assemble the pot.** Add the drained wheat and the chickpeas, stir, and arrange the potatoes and eggs on top.
4. **Add water.** Pour in boiling water to 3–4 cm above the wheat, salt and bring to a boil. Cook for 30 minutes over low heat and check that the wheat is still covered.
5. **Cook overnight.** Cover well and move to the oven at 100–110°C (212–230°F) for 10–12 hours. If it is very dry in the morning, add half a cup of boiling water at the side of the pot.
6. **Serve.** Peel the eggs and divide meat, wheat, potato and an egg into each serving.

### Variations and tips

* You can add a semolina kukla (dumpling), but it is not included in the calculation and adds about 180–250 calories.
* Don't leave the pot at a lukewarm temperature; it should stay above 60°C (140°F) throughout the night.
* Leftovers keep for 3 days in the fridge and freeze well without the eggs.

### Frequently asked questions

**Question: Are arisa and harissa the same thing?**
Answer: No. Arisa is a Libyan wheat Sabbath stew; harissa is a spicy pepper paste.

**Question: How many calories are in arisa?**
Answer: An eighth of the pot with meat, wheat, potato and an egg is about 690 calories.

**Question: Can you use bulgur?**
Answer: No. Bulgur is precooked and will fall apart overnight. You need whole wheat berries for hamin.

**Question: Why is the wheat dry in the morning?**
Answer: There was too little water, the lid wasn't tight, or the heat was too high. Start with liquid covering by 3–4 cm and an oven at 100–110°C.
