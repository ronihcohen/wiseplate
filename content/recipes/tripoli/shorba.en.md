## Libyan Shorba (Shorba Libiya)

*Shorba* simply means soup, but in Libya the name refers to the red soup of meat, [chickpeas](/en/food/chickpeas/) and small pasta, seasoned with harissa or hot pepper and balanced with dried mint and lemon. It is thicker than a clear soup but still eaten with a spoon, not as a stew.

**Prep time:** 15 minutes · **Cook time:** 75 minutes · **Total:** an hour and a half · **Yield:** 8 bowls · **Difficulty:** easy

### Why the pasta cooks in the soup

The orzo releases starch that thickens the liquid and absorbs the spices. That is also the trap: it keeps drinking liquid after the heat is off. If the soup is meant for tomorrow, cook the pasta separately and combine in each bowl.

### Ingredients (for 8 servings)

* 500 grams lean stewing [beef](/en/food/red-meat/), in small cubes
* 2 tablespoons [olive oil](/en/food/olive-oil/)
* 1 large [onion](/en/food/onion/), chopped
* 150 grams [tomato](/en/food/tomato/) paste
* 1 tablespoon harissa, or 1 teaspoon hot paprika
* 1 teaspoon turmeric, 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon, 1/2 teaspoon ground caraway
* 240 grams cooked chickpeas, drained
* 120 grams orzo, risoni ("tongues") pasta or rice-shaped Israeli pasta
* 2 liters hot water
* A handful of chopped parsley
* 1 tablespoon dried mint
* 3 tablespoons [lemon](/en/food/lemon/) juice, salt and pepper

### Choosing ingredients and substitutes

Beef shoulder cut into 1 cm cubes softens in a relatively short time; lamb works in the same amount. Commercial harissa varies a lot in heat and salt. Orzo is rice-shaped pasta and is found next to the other pasta; Israeli rice-shaped pasta works but cooks slightly faster.

### Method

1. **Brown the meat.** Heat oil in a pot and fry the cubes in a single layer until browned. Add the onion and fry for 5 minutes.
2. **Fry the paste.** Add the paste, harissa and spices and stir for 2–3 minutes until the paste is dark and starts to stick slightly.
3. **Cook until tender.** Pour in the water, scrape the bottom of the pot, add the chickpeas and bring to a boil. Cook partially covered for 40–50 minutes.
4. **Add the pasta.** Add the orzo and cook uncovered for 12–16 minutes, stirring especially often at the bottom.
5. **Balance at the end.** When the meat and pasta are tender, turn off the heat and add the parsley, mint and lemon. Taste for salt and heat and thin with boiling water if needed.

### Variations and tips

* For a vegetarian version, double the chickpeas and cook for 30 minutes; about 250 calories per bowl.
* The soup thickens a lot as it cools. Keep the pasta separate if making ahead.
* The mint and lemon go in after the heat is off to keep a fresh aroma.

### Frequently asked questions

**Question: How many calories are in shorba?**
Answer: A bowl of about 350 ml is about 310 calories, before bread.

**Question: What can you use instead of orzo?**
Answer: Small pasta such as risoni ("tongues") or rice-shaped Israeli pasta. Adjust the cooking time to the package.

**Question: Why did the soup turn into porridge the next day?**
Answer: The pasta kept absorbing liquid. Thin it with boiling water, or cook the pasta separately when making ahead.

**Question: Is harissa a must?**
Answer: No. A teaspoon of hot paprika and a little more garlic give a clean heat; harissa also adds spices and salt.
