## Editorial policy and sources

This page explains what the articles on [Wise Plate](/en/about/) are based on, and how they are checked and updated.

### Source priority

When several sources cover the same claim, articles rely on them in this order:

1. **Health and regulatory bodies** — fact sheets from the Israeli Ministry of Health, the US National Institutes of Health (NIH, especially the Office of Dietary Supplements), the European Food Safety Authority (EFSA) and the World Health Organization (WHO).
2. **Systematic reviews and meta-analyses**, and consensus positions of professional societies (for example the ISSN on sports supplements).
3. **Controlled clinical trials in humans.** Cell and animal studies are described as such, not as proof of an effect in people.

### Nutrition values

Nutrition values come mainly from the US Department of Agriculture's FoodData Central (USDA), and are checked against the labels of common products in Israel where they differ. Tables state the form of the food (raw, cooked, dried) and the serving size, because the differences are large.

### Doses and safety

In supplement articles, doses are given with their source, and next to the tolerable upper intake level where one is defined. Articles note known interactions with medications and groups who should be careful, and do not recommend personal doses.

### Balance

Every article has a downsides section, including for foods considered "healthy". When the evidence is weak, mixed or based on small studies, the article says so plainly instead of presenting it as fact.

### Updates and corrections

Articles are updated when new evidence is published or when a mistake is found. The date of the last update is shown at the top of every updated article. Mistakes can be reported through the [About page](/en/about/).

### What the site is not

The site does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment recommendations, and is not a substitute for a doctor or dietitian. For any question about a health condition, pregnancy, or combining supplements with medication, consult a professional.
