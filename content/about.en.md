## About Wise Plate

**Wise Plate (in Hebrew, "מדע בתנועה") is a knowledge base on nutrition, dietary supplements, running and recipes, in Hebrew and English.** The aim is to answer the questions people actually ask about what they eat — how much protein is in tofu, what the downsides of kefir are, which iron supplement is absorbed better — with a balanced answer that shows both the benefits and the downsides.

### What's on the site

* **[Food & nutrition](/en/food/)** — one article per food: nutrition facts, benefits, downsides, practical use and common questions.
* **[Supplements](/en/food/supplements/)** — vitamins, minerals and sports supplements: forms, doses, what the evidence shows and who should be careful.
* **[Comparisons](/en/compare/)** — similar foods side by side, with a practical conclusion.
* **[Recipes](/en/recipes/)** — traditional recipes from 12 cuisines, with calories per serving.
* **[Running & sport](/en/run/)** — heart rate zones, gear and navigation.

Every article is also available in Hebrew, which is the site's original language.

### Who it's for

Anyone who wants to understand what's on their plate and make informed choices, without hype and without scare stories. The site is not a substitute for personal medical or dietary advice — see the medical disclaimer at the foot of every page.

### How each article is made

Every article follows the same structure, so what you're looking for is easy to find: nutrition facts, benefits, downsides, practical use, a summary and common questions. The rules for choosing sources, checking numbers and updating articles are set out in the [editorial policy](/en/editorial-policy/).

### Funding

The site is free to read and shows Google AdSense ads, which Google selects automatically.
