They're small, but their reputation is huge. These three seeds are considered nutritional powerhouses, but each has different "operating instructions" for getting the most out of it.

### 1. [Chia Seeds](/en/food/chia-seeds/)

Tiny seeds from South America that were a staple food of Aztec warriors.

* **Composition:** Rich in soluble fiber, [calcium](/en/food/supplements/calcium/) and omega-3.
* **Benefits:**
    * **Liquid absorption:** They absorb up to 10-12 times their weight in water and form a gel. This property contributes to a long-lasting feeling of fullness and to keeping the body hydrated.
    * **[Calcium](/en/food/supplements/calcium/):** They contain more [calcium](/en/food/supplements/calcium/) than [milk](/en/food/milk/) (per 100 grams).
    * **Easy to use:** There's no need to grind them – the body can break down their coat and absorb all the goodness inside.
* **Downsides:**
    * **Price:** They can be relatively expensive.

### 2. [Flax Seeds](/en/food/flax-seeds/)

One of the oldest crops in the world, and the richest plant source of omega-3.

* **Composition:** The kings of omega-3 (ALA) and lignans.
* **Benefits:**
    * **Heart health:** Many studies show that regular consumption helps lower blood pressure and cholesterol.
    * **Lignans:** They contain phytoestrogens that may protect against hormonal cancers (such as breast and prostate cancer).
    * **Price:** Cheap and very widely available.
* **Downsides:**
    * **Must be ground:** Their hull is hard and indigestible. If you don't grind them, they simply "go in and come out" without any benefit.
    * **Oxidation:** They oxidize quickly after grinding, so they should be kept refrigerated.

### 3. Hemp Seeds

[Hemp seeds](/en/food/hemp-seeds/) (without the psychoactive compound), with a wonderful nutty flavor.

* **Composition:** The only seed that contains complete, high-quality protein in a significant amount.
* **Benefits:**
    * **Protein:** They contain all 9 essential amino acids, which makes them a perfect protein source for vegans and athletes.
    * **Taste:** A great nutty, rich flavor, soft and pleasant to chew (no cooking or soaking needed).
    * **Minerals:** A "powerhouse" of [magnesium](/en/food/supplements/magnesium/), [zinc](/en/food/supplements/zinc/) and [iron](/en/food/iron/).
* **Downsides:**
    * **Omega-6:** Very rich in omega-6 (relative to omega-3), although the ratio is considered balanced and healthy.
    * **Calories:** Calorie-dense.

### Nutritional comparison table (per tablespoon – 10 grams)

| Nutrient | [Chia seeds](/en/food/chia-seeds/) | [Flax seeds](/en/food/flax-seeds/) | Hemp seeds (hulled) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Calories** | ~49 | ~55 | ~57 |

| **Dietary fiber** | **Very high** | High | Low (in the seed's heart) |
| **How to use** | Eat as is / soak | **Must be ground!** | Eat as is |

### Summary and conclusions

For building muscle and an easy protein boost for a salad, **hemp seeds** are the tastiest and most effective choice. For heart health and hormonal balance, **[flax seeds](/en/food/flax-seeds/)** (ground!) are the cheap, natural wonder remedy. For fullness, regularity and a tasty pudding, **[chia seeds](/en/food/chia-seeds/)** are the undisputed winners.
