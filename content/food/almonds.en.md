## Almonds: Nutrient Density and Physiological Mechanisms

The almond (*Prunus dulcis*) is considered one of the most nutritionally dense foods. Botanically it is a drupe (stone fruit), but nutritionally it is classified as part of the nut family, characterized by a high concentration of monounsaturated fatty acids, plant protein and antioxidants.

### Research-based health benefits

* **Cardiovascular health:** Almonds are rich in oleic acid (MUFA), known for its ability to lower LDL cholesterol levels and maintain HDL levels ("good cholesterol"). Regular consumption is linked to a reduced risk of atherosclerosis.
* **[Vitamin E](/en/food/supplements/vitamin-e/) and antioxidants:** Almonds are among the best natural sources of vitamin E (in the form of alpha-tocopherol), a fat-soluble antioxidant that protects cell membranes from oxidative damage. Most of the antioxidants are concentrated in the thin brown skin.
* **Blood sugar regulation:** Thanks to their high [magnesium](/en/food/supplements/magnesium/) content, almonds help improve [insulin](/en/food/supplements/insulin/) sensitivity. Including almonds in a meal that contains carbohydrates lowers the meal's overall glycemic index and prevents sharp spikes in blood sugar.
* **Weight management and satiety:** Despite their calorie density, studies show that some of the energy in almonds isn't fully absorbed in the digestive system (because of the almond's cellular structure). In addition, the combination of protein and dietary fiber contributes to a long-lasting feeling of fullness.

### Downsides and health considerations

* **Phytic acid (phytates):** The almond skin contains phytic acid, which can bind to minerals such as [iron](/en/food/iron/), [calcium](/en/food/supplements/calcium/) and [zinc](/en/food/supplements/zinc/) and inhibit their absorption. Soaking almonds in water for several hours can reduce the phytic acid concentration and improve the bioavailability of the minerals.
* **High calorie density:** One gram of almonds contains about 6 calories. Uncontrolled consumption (free "snacking") can lead to a significant calorie surplus that will lead to weight gain.
* **Allergens:** Allergy to tree nuts, including almonds, is one of the most common and dangerous allergies (anaphylaxis). It requires complete avoidance by sensitive people.
* **Oxalates:** Almonds contain a relatively high amount of oxalates, substances that can contribute to kidney stone formation in people with a genetic predisposition or a medical history of [calcium](/en/food/supplements/calcium/) oxalate stones.

### Summary

The almond is a high-quality food that is an excellent source of healthy fats and micronutrients. Including a handful of natural almonds (unroasted and unsalted) in your daily diet offers significant benefits for the circulatory system and metabolism, as long as appropriate calorie control is maintained.

### Frequently asked questions

**Question: How many calories are in 10 almonds?**
Answer: 10 medium almonds contain about 70 calories.

**Question: Do you have to soak almonds before eating them?**
Answer: It isn't mandatory, but soaking can improve mineral absorption and ease digestion by reducing phytic acid.

**Question: Do almonds contain protein?**
Answer: Yes, almonds are an excellent source of plant protein, with about 21 grams of protein per 100 grams (about 6 grams per 30-gram serving).
