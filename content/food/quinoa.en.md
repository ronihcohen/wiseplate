## Nutritional and Health Benefits of Quinoa (Chenopodium quinoa)

Quinoa is a nutrient-dense pseudo-grain belonging to the amaranth family. Unlike classic grains, it is characterized by high-quality protein, a rich mineral profile and a proven contribution to metabolic and cardiovascular health.

### Complete protein and biological value

Quinoa contains about 13–15% protein (by dry weight) and is a rare plant source of complete protein, containing all the essential amino acids, in particular lysine – an amino acid that is limited in grains. This gives it high nutritional importance in a plant-based diet.

### Complex carbohydrates and glycemic balance

The carbohydrates in quinoa are mainly complex carbohydrates, accompanied by dietary fiber. Quinoa has a low-to-medium glycemic index, which helps regulate blood glucose levels, reduce glycemic load and keep you feeling full for longer.

### Dietary fiber and digestive health

Quinoa contains soluble and insoluble fiber that contribute to healthy bowel function, balanced cholesterol levels and support for the gut microbiome. Its fiber and resistant starch also help improve metabolic health.

### Essential micronutrients

Quinoa is a significant source of:

* [Magnesium](/en/food/supplements/magnesium/) – essential for nerve, muscle and enzyme activity
* Non-heme [iron](/en/food/iron/) – important for making hemoglobin and transporting oxygen
* [Zinc](/en/food/supplements/zinc/) – involved in immune function and metabolism
* [Potassium](/en/food/supplements/potassium/) and [phosphorus](/en/food/supplements/phosphorus/) – contribute to electrolyte balance and bone health
  It also contains B vitamins, in particular [folate](/en/food/supplements/vitamin-b9/).

### Bioactive compounds and antioxidants

Quinoa is rich in [polyphenols](/en/food/polyphenols/), flavonoids and [vitamin E](/en/food/supplements/vitamin-e/), which have antioxidant and anti-inflammatory activity. These compounds may reduce oxidative stress and contribute to the dietary prevention of chronic diseases.

### Contribution to heart and metabolic health

The combination of dietary fiber, [magnesium](/en/food/supplements/magnesium/) and antioxidants has been linked to an improved blood lipid profile, lower LDL cholesterol and support for blood pressure regulation.

### Unique nutritional aspects

Quinoa contains no [gluten](/en/food/gluten/), so it is suitable for people with celiac disease and gluten sensitivity. The seed coating contains saponins; rinsing before cooking reduces their content and improves bioavailability and taste.

### Summary

Quinoa is a functional food of especially high nutritional value, with a proven contribution to metabolic, cardiovascular and digestive health. Including it in a varied diet supports a balanced intake of protein, fiber and essential micronutrients.
