## Kiwi

**A medium green kiwi (about 75 g) provides roughly 45 calories, about 2.3 g of fiber and about 70 mg of vitamin C, nearly the whole daily requirement. Two green kiwis a day added more than one complete bowel movement a week for people with constipation in a multicenter controlled trial; the evidence for better sleep is preliminary. Kiwi is also a fairly common allergen.**

The kiwi (*Actinidia*, also called kiwifruit, in Hebrew "קיווי") originated in China and was developed commercially in New Zealand. There are two common types: **green kiwi** (*Actinidia deliciosa*, such as Hayward), tart and rich in fiber, and **gold kiwi** (*Actinidia chinensis*, such as SunGold), sweeter and even richer in vitamin C. Kiwi also contains actinidin, an enzyme that breaks down protein, which is why it tenderizes meat in a marinade and stops gelatin from setting.

### Benefits

* **Constipation: the strongest evidence.** In a multicenter trial in New Zealand, Japan and Italy, participants with functional constipation or constipation-predominant IBS ate two green kiwis a day for 4 weeks and, in another period, took psyllium. Kiwi added on average about one and a half complete spontaneous bowel movements a week, an improvement considered clinically meaningful, and improved abdominal comfort with no significant side effects. [Gearry et al., 2023](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36537785/). The mechanism probably combines fiber that holds a lot of water, actinidin and other compounds in the fruit.

* **Concentrated vitamin C.** One green kiwi provides close to the recommended intake for adults (75-90 mg), and a gold kiwi more. Vitamin C is essential for collagen formation and improves the absorption of iron from plant foods. [NIH ODS: Vitamin C](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminC-HealthProfessional/).

* **Sleep: preliminary evidence.** In a study in Taiwan, 24 adults with sleep problems ate two kiwis an hour before bed for 4 weeks. Time to fall asleep fell by about 35% and total sleep time rose by about 13%. [Lin et al., 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21669584/). There was no control group, so it is hard to separate the effect of the kiwi from the effect of taking part and keeping a routine. It is an interesting finding, not proof.

* **Nutrient density for few calories.** Kiwi delivers vitamin C, vitamin K, vitamin E, potassium and folate, all within about 45 calories per fruit.

### Downsides

* **Allergy.** Kiwi is one of the more prominent fruit allergens, with reactions ranging from an itchy mouth to systemic reactions, and young children may react more severely. [Bublin et al., 2013](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23394996/). Some people cross-react with latex, [banana](/en/food/banana/) and [avocado](/en/food/avocado/).

* **Tingling without allergy.** Actinidin and calcium oxalate crystals can cause mild tingling of the tongue, especially from unripe kiwi. That differs from allergy: there is no swelling and no shortness of breath.

* **Vitamin K and warfarin.** Anyone taking warfarin (Coumadin) should keep their intake of vitamin K foods steady and not suddenly start eating several kiwis a day. [NIH ODS: Vitamin K](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminK-HealthProfessional/).

* **Oxalates.** The amount is moderate, but people who form calcium oxalate kidney stones should ask about large quantities.

* **In the kitchen.** Actinidin breaks down protein: fresh kiwi in a gelatin dessert or in dairy dishes left to stand can turn bitter and runny.

### Practical use

* **For constipation:** two green kiwis a day, every day, for at least a few weeks. That is what the trial did.
* **With the skin:** green kiwi skin is edible after rubbing off the fuzz, and adds fiber. Gold kiwi skin is smooth.
* **Ripening:** a hard kiwi ripens at room temperature within a few days, faster in a bag next to an apple or banana.
* **Marinade:** mashed kiwi tenderizes meat in 15-30 minutes; any longer and the meat turns mushy.
* **In smoothies:** with [spinach](/en/food/spinach/), banana and yogurt; drink right away, before the enzyme changes the yogurt's texture.
* **With a plant-based meal:** kiwi's vitamin C improves absorption of [iron](/en/food/iron/) from legumes and grains.
* **Storage:** ripe kiwis keep for one to two weeks in the fridge; take them out a day ahead if you want them softer.

### Summary

Kiwi is a small fruit with plenty of vitamin C and fiber for under 50 calories. The strongest evidence for it concerns constipation: two green kiwis a day helped in a large controlled trial. The sleep findings are interesting but preliminary. People allergic to kiwi or latex need to be careful, and those taking warfarin need a consistent intake.

### Frequently asked questions

**Question: How many calories are in a kiwi?**
Answer: About 61 calories per 100 g, so roughly 45 calories for a medium green kiwi.

**Question: Does kiwi help with constipation?**
Answer: Yes. In a multicenter trial, two green kiwis a day for 4 weeks added about one and a half bowel movements a week and improved abdominal comfort.

**Question: Does kiwi help you sleep?**
Answer: A small study without a control group found faster sleep onset after two kiwis before bed. That is preliminary, but it is a reasonable evening snack anyway.

**Question: Is green or gold kiwi better?**
Answer: Gold is sweeter and richer in vitamin C. Most constipation studies used green kiwi.

**Question: Can you eat the skin?**
Answer: Yes. It adds fiber. Wash it and rub off the fuzz on green kiwi; gold kiwi skin is smooth.

**Question: Why does kiwi burn my mouth?**
Answer: Sometimes it is irritation from the enzyme and oxalate crystals. If you have swelling of the lips or throat, a rash or shortness of breath, it is probably an allergy and you should see a doctor.

**Question: Is kiwi safe in pregnancy?**
Answer: Yes, unless you have a known allergy. It provides vitamin C and folate.
