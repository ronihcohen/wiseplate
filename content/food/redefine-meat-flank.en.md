## Redefine Meat Flank Steak: A Scientific and Nutritional Analysis

Redefine Meat's **New Meat** technology represents a breakthrough in food engineering, attempting to recreate not only the taste but also the muscular, fibrous structure of beef using 3D printing and plant-based raw materials. Below is a scientific review of the product's nutritional benefits and downsides.

---

### Nutritional and health benefits

* **High protein content:** the product provides an impressive protein concentration of **25 grams per 100 grams**, a figure that matches and even exceeds some traditional beef cuts. The use of a combination of proteins (wheat, soy and potato) creates a broad amino acid profile.
* **No cholesterol:** unlike animal meat, which contains saturated fat and cholesterol linked to cardiovascular disease, this product contains **0 mg of cholesterol**, a significant advantage for heart health.
* **Dietary fiber:** natural beef contains no fiber at all. The plant-based flank contains **3 grams of fiber per 100 grams**, which contribute to satiety and support the digestive system.
* **Fatty acid profile:** the product is based on canola oil, which is rich in unsaturated fatty acids, and contains a relatively low amount of saturated fat (1.5 grams) compared with fatty beef cuts.

### Downsides and health considerations

* **Ultra-processed food (UPF):** the product goes through complex manufacturing processes and has a long ingredient list, including maltodextrin, modified starches and flavorings. High consumption of processed food has been linked in studies to an increased risk of metabolic problems.
* **[Sodium](/en/food/supplements/sodium/) level:** the product contains **300 mg of [sodium](/en/food/supplements/sodium/) per 100 grams**. Although this is a reasonable figure compared with other meat substitutes, it is still significantly higher than an unprocessed fresh cut of beef, which calls for attention from people with high blood pressure.
* **Allergens:** the product contains **[gluten](/en/food/gluten/)** (from wheat and [barley](/en/food/barley/)) and **soy**, two of the most common allergens, which makes it unsuitable for people with celiac disease or these sensitivities.
* **Mineral absorption:** unlike [red meat](/en/food/red-meat/), which is a natural and highly available source of [iron](/en/food/iron/) (heme [iron](/en/food/iron/)) and vitamins, in the plant product mineral availability may be lower because of the phytates (absorption inhibitors) found naturally in plant protein.

---

### Summary and recommendations

Redefine Meat's flank steak is an impressive technological alternative that provides quality protein without the cholesterol effects of meat. However, since it is a processed product with a certain [sodium](/en/food/supplements/sodium/) content, it is best included as part of a balanced diet that is also based on whole, unprocessed foods.

The product seems ideal for "reducetarians" and vegans looking for the sensory experience of meat without the ethical or environmental consequences of the animal industry.
