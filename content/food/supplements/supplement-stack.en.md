## Introduction: What Is a "Stack"?

In the world of dietary supplements, the term "stack" refers to a strategic combination of several supplements taken together or over the course of the day to achieve a particular health goal (such as building muscle, improving metabolism or general health). The idea is that some supplements work better together ("synergy"), while others may interfere with each other.
In this guide we'll learn how to combine the 9 supplements we've reviewed on the site: **[creatine](/en/food/supplements/creatine/), protein, [omega-3](/en/food/supplements/omega-3/), [magnesium](/en/food/supplements/magnesium/), [caffeine](/en/food/supplements/caffeine/), [berberine](/en/food/supplements/berberine/), [zinc](/en/food/supplements/zinc/), [vitamin D](/en/food/supplements/vitamin-d/) and [turmeric](/en/food/supplements/turmeric/).**

## The [Iron](/en/food/iron/) Rules of Combining

### 1. Winning combinations (Synergies)
*   **[Vitamin D](/en/food/supplements/vitamin-d/) + [magnesium](/en/food/supplements/magnesium/):** vitamin D needs magnesium to be converted into its active form. Taking them together (or on the same day) is essential.
*   **[Turmeric](/en/food/supplements/turmeric/) + fat:** curcumin is fat-soluble. Combining it with [omega-3](/en/food/supplements/omega-3/) or a fatty meal will significantly improve absorption.
*   **[Zinc](/en/food/supplements/zinc/) + protein:** animal protein improves the absorption of zinc.
*   **[Caffeine](/en/food/supplements/caffeine/) + training:** the classic combination for improving performance.

### 2. Problematic combinations (Antagonists)
*   **Zinc + magnesium + [calcium](/en/food/supplements/calcium/):** at very high doses (above standard), these minerals may compete for absorption. However, at normal doses (as in ZMA), the body manages. Better to split them if you take high therapeutic doses.
*   **Caffeine + a night's sleep:** not a chemical combination but a biological one. Caffeine stays in the blood for many hours. The rule: stop caffeine 8 hours before bed.
*   **Dietary fiber + everything:** fiber (as in a psyllium supplement) can "bind" supplements and interfere with their absorption. Keep supplements away from especially high-fiber meals.

## Sample Daily Schedule: The Full Stack

Here's how you can combine all 9 supplements in one day, in a way that maximizes absorption and avoids clashes:

### 🌞 Morning (with breakfast)
Morning is a great time for supplements that provide energy or need food to be absorbed.
*   **Vitamin D:** needs fat to be absorbed.
*   **Omega-3:** needs fat to be absorbed (great with vitamin D).
*   **Turmeric:** needs fat (goes great with the omega-3).
*   **[Berberine](/en/food/supplements/berberine/) (dose 1):** to improve [insulin](/en/food/supplements/insulin/) sensitivity at the meal.
*   **Multivitamin / zinc:** if you take them separately (zinc not on an empty stomach!).

### ⚡ Before training (Pre-Workout)
*   **Caffeine:** about 30-60 minutes before training for alertness.
*   **[Creatine](/en/food/supplements/creatine/):** can be taken before or after; the timing is less critical, but it's convenient to add to a shake.

### 💪 After training (Post-Workout)
*   **Whey protein** (a by-product of [cheese](/en/food/cheese/) making): for fast recovery and muscle building.

### 🍽️ Lunch / early evening
*   **Berberine (dose 2):** before the biggest meal of the day (carbohydrates).

### 🌙 Night (before bed)
Time to wind down and let the body recover.
*   **Magnesium:** for muscle relaxation and better sleep (magnesium glycinate is recommended).
*   **Avoid:** caffeine or vitamin D (which may interfere with melatonin production in some people) at these hours.

## Summary and Personalization

Not everyone needs **all** the supplements.
*   **For athletes:** the emphasis is on creatine, protein, caffeine and magnesium.
*   **For general health ("anti-aging"):** the emphasis is on vitamin D, omega-3, turmeric and berberine.
*   **For immune support:** zinc, vitamin D and [vitamin C](/en/food/supplements/vitamin-c/) (not reviewed here, but recommended).

Listen to your body. Start with one or two supplements, and see how you respond before you build a whole "tower" of pills.

> [!WARNING]
> **Medical note:** this guide is for information only. If you take prescription medications (especially blood thinners, diabetes or heart medications), you must consult a doctor before combining supplements such as berberine, turmeric or omega-3.
