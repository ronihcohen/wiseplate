## Nutritional and Health Benefits of Brazil Nuts (Bertholletia excelsa)

Brazil nuts (in Hebrew "אגוזי ברזיל") are large, rich seeds from a tree that grows in the Amazon rainforest. They are famous mainly for being the most concentrated dietary source in nature of the mineral [selenium](/en/food/supplements/selenium/).

### A super source of [selenium](/en/food/supplements/selenium/)

A single Brazil nut may provide more than 100% of the recommended daily intake of [selenium](/en/food/supplements/selenium/). [Selenium](/en/food/supplements/selenium/) is an essential trace mineral that serves as a cofactor for antioxidant enzymes (glutathione peroxidase) and is critical for proper immune function.

### Thyroid support

The thyroid gland has the highest concentration of [selenium](/en/food/supplements/selenium/) in the body, and it is needed to produce thyroid hormones (T3) and to protect the gland from oxidative damage. Eating Brazil nuts may help prevent an underactive thyroid and related autoimmune conditions.

### Healthy fats

Brazil nuts are rich in monounsaturated fatty acids (similar to [olive oil](/en/food/olive-oil/)) and polyunsaturated fatty acids. These fats are essential for heart health and for lowering "bad" cholesterol levels.

### Antioxidants and anti-inflammatory properties

In addition to [selenium](/en/food/supplements/selenium/), the nuts contain [vitamin E](/en/food/supplements/vitamin-e/) (tocopherol) and phenols such as ellagic acid, which work together to reduce inflammation in the body and protect against oxidative stress.

### Cognitive function

Adequate [selenium](/en/food/supplements/selenium/) levels are linked to improved brain function and prevention of cognitive decline. The nut's antioxidant properties may protect brain cells from damage.

### Summary

Brazil nuts are a nutritional powerhouse, providing first and foremost essential [selenium](/en/food/supplements/selenium/). Because of the high concentration, it is recommended to eat them in moderation (1–3 nuts a day) to enjoy their benefits for the thyroid, heart and immune system without worrying about excess [selenium](/en/food/supplements/selenium/).

### Frequently asked questions

**Question: How much [selenium](/en/food/supplements/selenium/) is in one Brazil nut?**
Answer: One nut can contain between 68 and 91 micrograms of [selenium](/en/food/supplements/selenium/), which is over 100% of the recommended daily intake for an adult.

**Question: How many Brazil nuts can you eat a day?**
Answer: The recommended amount is 1-2 nuts a day. Regularly eating more than 3-4 nuts may lead to [selenium](/en/food/supplements/selenium/) toxicity (selenosis).

**Question: Are Brazil nuts radioactive?**
Answer: They naturally contain a tiny amount of radium (because of their root system), but the amount is negligible and not dangerous when eaten in moderation.
